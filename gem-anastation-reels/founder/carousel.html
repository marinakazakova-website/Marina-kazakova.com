Founder Story

I was the first in my family
to choose jewelry making.

As a student, I sometimes had just enough money for food and jewelry tools. But I was genuinely happy. I knew I had found my path.

I started as a jeweler.
Then I became
a gemologist.

Ten years in jewelry production. Then a move to Bangkok and five years working with major companies in Asia. Back then, I never imagined I would one day create my own brand.

We are all
a work in progress.

I believe we spend our entire lives learning and refining our talents. Just like every gemstone, every person is unique.

ANASTASIA
Founder, Gem AnaStation
